          How to use

          Pair in one tap

          On the viewing device: + Add device. On the camera device either scan the QR with the iPhone Camera app, type the 6-character code under Pair with code, or tap a shared link. Tap Approve: the camera starts and the viewer opens the live feed by itself. Both devices remember each other, so next time just open Sentinel.

          Stay connected

          Each feed reconnects on its own (1 s, 2 s, 4 s … up to every 30 s, forever) and immediately when Wi-Fi returns or you reopen the app. A heartbeat checks the link every 2 s; if the network changes, Sentinel restores the path (ICE restart) without dropping the call. LIVE (red) means new frames in the last 3 s. STALE means connected but frozen. Some strict networks (certain cellular / hotel Wi-Fi) need a TURN relay: add your own in Settings → ICE servers.

          Keep screen awake

          With Keep screen awake on, the screen stays on while you monitor or stream (Screen Wake Lock, iOS 16.4+; older iOS uses a silent video trick that needs one tap). iOS releases it whenever you leave Sentinel; it comes back on its own when you return. For all-day use, also plug in and set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.

          Guard mode (screen lock)

          Remote start: on the camera device tap STANDBY · REMOTE START and leave Sentinel open (plugged in). On the monitor tap START MONITORING: the camera wakes, turns on and streams. Stop cameras sends it back to standby. The gear icon changes front/back camera, resolution, quality, fps, mic, sound, keep-awake, guard and darkness on the camera, and shows the camera's confirmation. Only paired monitors holding that device's secret are obeyed.

          Tap the lock icon (or Guard mode on a station). The screen goes black and ignores touches, so pockets, kids or pets cannot press anything, while video, recording and wake lock keep running. Press and hold the ring for 2 seconds to unlock, or set a PIN in Settings. Alerts still sound and show a line on the guard screen.

          iOS limit: pressing the side button / the real iPhone lock screen, switching apps, or Control Center pauses the camera and microphone. Apple does this for every web app and it cannot be overridden. Use Guard mode instead of locking the phone. Sentinel restarts the camera and reconnects when you return.

          Privacy

          Video goes device to device, encrypted (WebRTC DTLS-SRTP). The broker only introduces devices. Only devices you approved, holding a per-device secret, can view a station. Nothing is uploaded.
